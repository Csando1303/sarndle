Privacy & cookies

Last updated 17 November 2026

The short version

Sarndle has no accounts and doesn't ask for your name or email. Your game progress stays in your own browser. The site shows ads from Google, and Google may use cookies for them. If you're in the UK or EU, you'll be asked first.

What your browser stores

To make the game work, Sarndle saves a few things in your browser's local storage: today's guesses, your stats and streak, whether you've seen the "How to play" screen, and the colour-blind setting. This never leaves your device and isn't sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Advertising

Sarndle uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technology to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites, and to measure how ads perform. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the UK, EEA and Switzerland see a consent message before any personalised ads are shown. You can turn personalised ads off at any time in Google's ad settings.

Server logs

Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard logs (such as IP address, browser type and the time of each request) to keep the site running and secure.
