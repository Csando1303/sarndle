How to play

Guess today's UK supermarket sandwich in 6 tries. Start typing and pick a sandwich from the list.

  • 🟩 Green: exactly right
  • 🟨 Yellow: close. Price within 50p, some fillings in common, or a similar kind of bread
  • ⬛ Grey: wrong
  • ↑ ↓ The real price is higher or lower

Clues: 🏪 Supermarket · 💷 Price · 🥓 Filling · 🍞 Bread · 🏷️ Range (Value, Standard, Premium, Festive, Plant-based or Free From)

Stuck? After 3 guesses you can reveal a hint.

Missed a day? Tap 📅 to play past sarnies from the archive.

A new sarnie arrives every day at midnight (UK time). Prices come from supermarket websites and may differ in store. Sarndle isn't affiliated with any supermarket.
